What is the significance of the 1994 digital manifestos?

robot_2 AI-Assisted Synthesis

The 1994 manifestos represented a pivotal shift in understanding digital permanence. Authors argued against the ephemeral nature of early web technologies, proposing structural frameworks that eventually led to modern archival standards. They are considered foundational texts in digital heritage preservation.

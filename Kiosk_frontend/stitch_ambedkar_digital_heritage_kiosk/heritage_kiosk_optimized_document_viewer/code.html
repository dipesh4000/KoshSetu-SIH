Annihilation of Caste (Draft)

Date: 1936 Archive ID: ADH-TXT-36-001 Provenance: Rajgruha Collection

Play Narrated Transcript

0:00 4:23

If you wish to bring about a breach in the system then you have got to apply the dynamite to the Vedas and the Shastras, which deny any part to reason, to Vedas and Shastras, which deny any part to morality.

You must destroy the Religion of the Smritis. Nothing else will avail. This is my considered view of the matter.

Whether you do it or not, it is for you to consider.
